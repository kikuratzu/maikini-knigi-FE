Book of the month

Time Shelter

by Georgi Gospodinov

A clinic in Zurich recreates the past, decade by decade, to help patients with memory loss — until whole countries start voting on which decade to go back to. Funny, tender and unsettling, the 2023 International Booker Prize winner by one of Bulgaria's finest writers.

About us

A bookshop that feels like home

Maikini Knigi — “Mum's books” — began with one family's library and a shelf by the window. Today it's a cosy shop with armchairs, strong coffee and a reading club every other Thursday.

  • Carefully curated

    Every book is chosen by our booksellers. No algorithms, just people who read.

  • Gift wrapping

    Add a handwritten note and we'll wrap your book in our kraft paper for free.

  • Fast delivery

    Delivery across Bulgaria in 1–3 working days. Free on orders over €40.

  • Reading club

    Join us every other Thursday at 19:00 to talk about the book of the month.

New arrivals, once a month

Our favourite new books, events and the occasional discount. No spam, ever.
